How it works

The chip hears, the FPGA cleans, the AI understands

input

Microphone

Preamp and sample-and-hold on the board.

our chip

8-bit SAR ADC

2 MS/s, designed for SCL 1.2 µm.

Basys3 FPGA

Oversampling filter

CIC + FIR to 16 kHz audio, about 10-bit quality.

ESP32-S3

Keyword AI

Log-mel features and a 21.7k-parameter CNN.

output

Action

10 commands, no internet needed.

The trick is oversampling. Sampling 125× faster than the audio rate and filtering on the FPGA turns a cheap 8-bit chip into voice-quality audio: about 10 bits in the voice band.
